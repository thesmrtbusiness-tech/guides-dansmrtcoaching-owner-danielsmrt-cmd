Why It Stopped Working

The scale isn't broken. The plan was too heavy to carry.

Most people who land here have already cycled through 2–4 diets — keto, Noom, intermittent fasting, calorie counting. Most of them worked, for a while. What actually failed wasn't your discipline — it was the mental overhead of sustaining a rigid system through a season of life that's already full: a demanding job, kids, aging parents, not enough sleep.

You don't need to track harder. You need a version of "eating well" that still works on the weeks you don't have the bandwidth to think about food at all.
